iDNA Applications comes with a complete and fully defined set of design
rulesets, insights and patterns that give you a good overview of design
issues and challenges to take into account while modernizing or
transforming applications. Still, most organization will have additional
or specific things they would like to search for. Therefore, iDNA
Applications comes with an option to define your own custom Design
Insights. This can be done on the Custom Insights – Manage page.

Please also refer to [Custom Insights](/kb/custom-insights/) to
understand how the interface of the "Custom Insights - Manage" section
works.
