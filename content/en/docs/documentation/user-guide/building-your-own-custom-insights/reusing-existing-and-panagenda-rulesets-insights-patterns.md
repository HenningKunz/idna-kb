iDNA Applications comes with preset rulesets, Insights and Patterns.
These cannot be changed. panagenda elements can be recognized because
they have a panagenda logo behind their name in the Custom Insights
page.

You can create a duplicate of a ruleset, insight or pattern (both your
own as well as those provided by panagenda) by clicking the copy icon
behind panagenda logo and name of the element on the Custom Insights
page. 

{{% callout type="info" %}}

**Using the copy option on either a ruleset or insight will also create
new copies of all underlying insights and patterns. It is therefore wise
to really think through what is needed. Having loads of copies of
existing insights and patterns might be confusing to manage and it might
be better to create a new insight or Ruleset and reuse the existing
patterns in combination with your new ones.**
